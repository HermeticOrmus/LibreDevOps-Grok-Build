# Infra Cost Scan

Find expensive smells, not fake savings theater.

## Steps
1. Identify always-on vs burst workloads.
2. Flag idle resources, oversized instances, orphan disks.
3. Check storage lifecycle and log retention.
4. Propose measured experiments before large cuts.
